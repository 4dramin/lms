1 · Import a document

Documents are processed in this browser. For DOCX, PPTX and text-based PDFs, questions are extracted using the built-in parser. PDF scans require OCR or manual entry; embedded animations and complex layout may not survive conversion. You must review and set correct answers.

Privacy / new assessment: No sample exam, previously imported questions, student password or school is preloaded. Importing a new document replaces the current question bank by default.
New assessment: no questions, passwords or previous exam content loaded.
How to format documents for reliable automatic detection

Write numbered questions (e.g. 1. What is the answer?), choices A) ... B) ... C) ..., and an answer key such as Answer: B. The extractor also recognizes Correct: .... It cannot reliably infer answers from images, typography or underlining.

5 · Validate and export

All questions need a correct answer, two or more choices, and positive marks. No accidental zero-score assessments. SCORM writes raw and maximum score (SCORM 2004 also writes scaled score), completion and pass/fail status. It commits the attempt when submitted.

Have you checked the correct answers?

Automatically identified answers are suggestions, not guaranteed facts. Check every option against the original document before exporting.
